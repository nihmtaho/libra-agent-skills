# Onboarding drop-off

## Problem
New workspaces stop at the invite step and leave.

## Options considered
1. Pre-fill a demo workspace — cost: a day; assumes: an empty product is the reason.
2. Ask for invites during sign-up — cost: two days; assumes: intent exists at sign-up.

## Chosen direction
Option 2, because it is cheap.

## Riskiest assumption
That people want to invite anyone at sign-up.

## Cheapest test
Ship it and watch the number.
